# OpenAI-compatible `/v1/*` passthrough relay

Added a catch-all relay under `/v1/*` so Unaiter forwards whatever path the
upstream exposes (embeddings, moderations, rerank, and similar), after rewriting
`{providerSlug}/{modelId}` and rotating Roundabout keys on 429/402. Only
`GET /v1/models` stays Unaiter-owned (enabled catalogue). Chat completions keep
their dedicated handler for the intent router and provider adapters; everything
else passes through.
