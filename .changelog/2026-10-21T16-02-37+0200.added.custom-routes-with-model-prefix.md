Moved the intent planner UI and config API from `/router` to `/auto`, and made `/router` the home for named custom routes. You can attach catalogue models (filterable by all/enabled/disabled) under a slug prefix such as `cursor/…`; enabled attachments appear on `GET /v1/models` and chat/passthrough simply strip the prefix before proxying. Overview counts show enabled versus total attached, and enabled drops when a model is removed from its provider.
